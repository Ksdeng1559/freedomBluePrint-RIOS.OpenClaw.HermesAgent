# 02 — Business Interpretation and Context Model

**Inputs:** evidence_bundle, intake_context, approved Client Brain, verified prior CRM outcomes.
**Process:** interpret what the company does, customer segments, probable objectives, constraints and assumptions; map service/product fit and ecosystem relationships. Clearly separate verified facts, reasonable hypotheses and unknowns. Do not score or draft outreach yet.
**Outputs:** `business_identity_profile`, `objective_scorecard_inputs`, `constraint_profile`, `relationship_opportunity_map`, `context_model`.
**Evidence:** source references for every material claim and explicit confidence.
**Success:** business context explains *why* an opportunity may exist, not merely who the company is.
**Allowed tools:** evidence read, bounded interpretation, tenant-scoped context write.
**Review gate:** independent checks of unsupported assumptions and sensitive inferences.
**Escalation:** high uncertainty requires more research, not hallucinated context.

# 03 — Signal and Relationship Interpretation

**Inputs:** approved context_model, evidence_bundle, client solution catalog, feedback snapshot.
**Process:** identify and date structured signals within relevant 90/180-day or client-approved windows; interpret business impact, timing, probable constraint and relationship pathway. Map problem -> solution -> relationship -> opportunity. Separate confirmed triggers from hypothetical buying intent.
**Outputs:** `interpreted_signals`, `ecosystem_graph`, `relationship_opportunity_map`, `why_now_evidence`.
**Evidence:** event date, observed_at, source reference, confidence and freshness.
**Success:** each candidate signal has a business interpretation and a relevance thesis.
**Allowed tools:** bounded signal reasoning and evidence retrieval.
**Review gate:** check signal validity, freshness and unsupported inference.
**Escalation:** no supported signal -> classify as exploratory, not urgent.
**Next:** `04_decisions`.
